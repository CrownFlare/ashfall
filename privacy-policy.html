Privacy Policy — Vaelareth

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Vaelareth ("the game", "the app") is a single-player, offline role-playing game developed by Jereneo Joven ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It runs entirely on your device and makes no network connections.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or usage information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device only (in the app's local storage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently removes it.

In-app purchases

The app offers in-app purchases. These are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — Google handles all payment processing. Records of what you have purchased are used only to unlock the corresponding content on your device. Purchases are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.

Third-party sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only third party involved is Google Play, solely to process in-app purchases as described above.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage-access permissions).

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a teen and older audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised "Last updated" date.
